As built (round 2): desktop only; the Phone width is out of scope (FEEDBACK_R2_PLAN E7). Where this file and composer.html differ, composer.html wins.

The other side never shows as "writing": one steady line says whose move it is. A decision card holds Decline and Approve and send. The box at the bottom has two modes. Comment talks to your team, and no Claude reads it. A finished conversation reads Closed and can be reopened.

Decide, comment, or edit

The card holds the decision: Approve and send, or Decline on the left. To fix a word yourself, click into the draft.

The box at the bottom is for everything else, in one of two modes: Comment to your team, or Edit to tell Claude what to do differently.

The question and the whole draft show in full: no "View more", no inner scroll.

Closed, not expired

However a conversation ends (a side closed it, both went quiet, or it hit its turn limit or deadline), it reads Closed, with the reason in a few words.

Reopen sits right under it, for either side. It opens a small prompt to continue the discussion (next tab).

Edit is shown but off, with the hint "Reopen the conversation to tell Claude what to pick up": Edit on a closed conversation is refused, and Reopen is how Claude picks it up.

Before: "Expired. It ended without a close: both sides went quiet, or it reached its turn limit or deadline." with no way back in.
